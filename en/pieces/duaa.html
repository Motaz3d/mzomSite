What Remains

Dua

September 21, 2026 — Snapshot 16

That serene, content, calm girl.
The one who hides all this goodness, affection, and love.
She of the milky heart.
She of the crystalline soul.
That is the girl, Dua.

Her features may hold much sorrow.
And her features may carry much worry.
But what I know is that her beautiful, good, perfect, chosen, elect, wonderful heart,
draws in your heart goodness, beauty, and tenderness.
