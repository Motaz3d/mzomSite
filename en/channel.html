Economics as a social science

The economy is not factories that produce. The economy is life, law, and a way of living.

A channel documenting people's economic life as it is actually lived: how do goods travel from the port to the street market to cargo to the airplane? How does the worker live, and how does the decision-maker decide? We do not explain the economy in theory — we show it to the eye: a real person, a real place, goods on the move.
