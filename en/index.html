Everyday Estrangement

Tirat Haifa

September 11, 2026 — Snapshot 6

Perhaps God gave me things, and things; He gave me truthfulness.

I went to our original village in Tirat Haifa. I walked through it, and it was as though it were saying to me: Where are my people? Where are the human beings who were here? Where are they?

I found empty lands, with no human being in them.

So I said to myself: I must go back to my homeland, to my country.

But is the country I was born in my country?
