What Remains

To Sarah

September 20, 2026 — Snapshot 15

To my beloved:
To my princess:
To my queen:
To Sarah:

The most beautiful day of my life is when I see your name beside mine on an official document called marriage. After my soul had been beside your soul, and when my heart was beside your heart, and when my self was with your self, my name came to rest upon your name — husband and wife.

The most beautiful moments of life are meeting your beloved, and more beautiful than the most beautiful moments of life is to be bound to him. More beautiful than the most beautiful thing in the world is to look into your eyes, my beloved, my queen, hymn of my poetry, my first and last love, my love deep and visible.

To you I offer my heart, my self, and my soul through my words. Do you know what I yearn for? I yearn only that your eyes may read my words. When your eyes read my words, I become passion and ardour, and everything within everything.

I gift you my joy upon a plate of passion, and I gift you my love upon a plate of happiness, and I gift Motaz to Sarah.
